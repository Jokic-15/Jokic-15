I am currently an M.Sc. in Chemistry (by Research) at the National University of Singapore (NUS), Faculty of Science, advised by Prof. Xiaoyan Li. My research focuses on computational chemistry — using DFT and genetic algorithms to understand surface reconstruction and reaction mechanisms on alloy catalysts, as well as electrocatalytic C–N coupling for amino acid synthesis. Before joining NUS, I completed my B.Sc. in Chemistry (Sino-Foreign Cooperative Education) at Northeast Forestry University (NEFU), and spent time as a visiting student at Westlake University with Prof. Hongyu Chen, working on morphology control of gold nanostars for photothermal cancer therapy.
